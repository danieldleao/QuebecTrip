How to read the prices

Exact Dec 27–Jan 1 rates aren't published this far out — they need a live date query. Nightly rates below are estimates for New Year's peak week (benchmarked against December averages and current "from" prices), except Ferme St-Adolphe which publishes real rates. Taxes estimated at ~18% (GST 5% + QST 9.975% + lodging tax ~3.5%). Airbnb adds ~14% service fee + ~18.5% Quebec taxes on top of nightly rates. Hotel totals = room + tax + parking (1 car), breakfast noted separately. Rental totals = nightly + cleaning + platform fees + tax.

Budget verdict

💰 Absolute cheapest: Motel Lévis (~C$1,298)

Nothing beats it on price — ~70% less than the cheapest downtown hotel. But be honest about the trade: no breakfast, no pool, 7.2/10 reviews, 30+ min winter drives with a 6-year-old, and NYE bridge/ferry logistics. Add C$300–400 for breakfasts out and the gap to #2 nearly vanishes.

✅ Cheapest I'd actually book: Econo Lodge Airport (~C$1,652)

Free breakfast for 8, free parking (even for 2 cars), connecting rooms, kids-stay-free. ~C$207/person for 5 nights over New Year's is remarkable, and it's the cheapest option that doesn't punish you daily.

🏊 Cheapest that keeps the kids happy: Super 8 (~C$2,360)

The 80-ft waterslide + free breakfast + in-room microwave/fridge is the strongest family package under C$2,500. Worth the ~C$700 over Econo Lodge if pool time matters at −15°C. Still ~C$1,900 cheaper than the cheapest Old Quebec hotel.

🏠 Cheapest togetherness: Ferme St-Adolphe chalet (~C$2,240)

The only verified sub-C$2,300 rental where all 8 share living space and a kitchen — published, taxes-included, fee-free direct pricing. If cooking together on NYE matters more than a pool, this is it. Tight at 3 BR for 8, and mountain roads daily.

Winter & booking caveats

  • Driving: Sainte-Foy options = 15–20 min via autoroute; Old Quebec parking runs C$25–39/day if you drive in daily. Lévis options use the Pierre-Laporte bridge or the Québec–Lévis ferry (runs year-round — check holiday/late-night schedule, critical for NYE).
  • Occupancy: the family group (3 adults + 2 kids = 5 in one hotel room) exceeds standard quad occupancy everywhere — confirm rollaway/extra-person policy when booking. Super 8 publishes C$20/nt extra-adult fee.
  • Minimum stays: chalets commonly require 4–5 nights Dec 21–Jan 7 — your 5 nights satisfies this. Direct chalets often take 35% non-refundable deposits.
  • Book early: New Year's week is peak season; get live quotes for your exact dates before treating any estimate as a price.
