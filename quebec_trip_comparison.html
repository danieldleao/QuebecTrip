Family Strategy: Keeping 6-Year-Olds & 13-Year-Old Happy

Balancing energy levels, winter temperatures, and bedtime routines across the two family groups.

🧸 For the Two 6-Year-Olds

Short outdoor bursts (1.5–2 hours max) at the Dufferin Terrace Toboggan slide and Place D'Youville ice rink. Afternoons dedicated to the heated pool or Valcartier indoor Bora Parc. Early bedtime at 8:00 PM without disturbing the adults in a chalet.

🏂 For the 13-Year-Old

High-speed adventure at Village Vacances Valcartier's 35+ snow slides, night skiing at Stoneham (under lights until 9:30 PM), exploring the underground fortifications, and participating in the Grande Allée New Year's outdoor music countdown.

🥂 For the 5 Adults

A stress-free New Year's Eve celebration: either a shared gourmet dinner by the chalet fireplace and outdoor hot tub, or taking an Uber/shuttle into Old Quebec while kids are supervised, avoiding high holiday restaurant cover charges.
